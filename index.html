Does Malik Cattle & Farmhouse sell cattle?

The supplied Facebook identity is presented here primarily as a property / land business. “Cattle farm” is a promoted use of the land, not an animal catalogue on this website.

What plot sizes are promoted?

1,000, 2,000 and 4,000 sq yd appear prominently in public promotions, with 3,000 sq yd also appearing in marketing material. Confirm current availability before planning a visit.

What is the current price?

Old promotional prices are not shown as current. Ask directly by call or WhatsApp for the latest quote.

Is WhatsApp available?

Yes. Use the WhatsApp buttons on this website to contact the primary number 0334 2764521 for current availability, pricing and site-visit details.

Can I visit the site?

Contact the team first to confirm the current map pin, timing and the plots available to view.

Are all photos on this website actual project photos?

No. Images marked “Concept visual” are high-resolution presentation images used to communicate possible land and farmhouse uses. For current real project media, use the official Facebook feed/page.

Are legal approvals or possession guaranteed?

No unsupported legal or possession guarantee is made here. Ask for current documents and independently verify them before payment.
